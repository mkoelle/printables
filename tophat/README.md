# 3D-printed top hat with hidden electronics cage

A parametric top hat for a 220 x 220 mm bed, printed in PLA. It hides a mounting
cage for storage and IoT devices above the wearer's head, invisible from
outside. Everything is generated by Python scripts run inside FreeCAD 1.1.

## Parts

| Part | Model | STL | Notes |
|------|-------|-----|-------|
| Crown | `TopHat.FCStd` | `stl/tophat_crown.stl` | Prints upside down, turned 45 deg. Has snap groove, rib slots and wedge lip inside |
| Brim (`BrimSegs` segments, 4 on a 220 mm bed) | `TopHat.FCStd` | `stl/tophat_brimq1..N.stl` | Dovetail seams, glued. Inner collar takes the crown. Sides curl up |
| Hatband | `TopHat.FCStd` | `stl/tophat_hatband.stl` | Slides on from the crown's bottom end before the crown meets the brim |
| Cage | `CageFingers.FCStd` | `stl/tophat_cage_fingers.stl` | Bottom ring + 6 spring fingers, each with a toothed dovetail rail |
| Rail sliders | `rail.py` | (in rail tests) | Click along rails in 2.4 mm steps; device holders will snap onto them |

Assembly: glue brim segments; slide hatband on; squeeze the cage finger tips
and push the cage up through the crown's bottom opening; snap the crown onto
the brim collar. The crown's wedge lip then presses the finger tips to the
wall.

## Files

| File | Role |
|------|------|
| `tophat_param.py` | Builds `TopHat` (crown, brim, hatband, lip) from the `Params` spreadsheet. Parameter defaults live here |
| `cage.py` | Builds `CageFingers` from TopHat's Params; rail count, finger width, head dome clearance |
| `rail.py` | Shared rail and slider geometry (imported by `cage.py` and `test_kit.py`) |
| `export_stl.py` | Exports hat parts, oriented for printing |
| `test_kit.py` | Builds the test models: `TopHatFitTests` (head fit), `FixtureTests` (joints), `RailTests` |
| `build_all.py` | Runs everything above in order |
| `TESTING.md` | What each test print checks and which parameter to change |
| `stl/round1/` | Copy of the first test prints (head fit, tolerance, rail) |

## Parameters

`Params` spreadsheet in `TopHat.FCStd`, three sections:

- **Main**: `HeadCirc`, `Oval`, `CrownH`, `Flare`, `BrimW`, `Curl`, `Nozzle`,
  `WallPerims`, `Clr`, `Bed`. `HeadCirc` comes from `SIZE` at the top of
  `tophat_param.py`: a preset (XS 530, S 550, M 570, L 590, XL 610, XXL 630 mm)
  or a measured circumference in mm.
- **Advanced**: formulas from Main, overridable (brim, collar, snap, ribs,
  dovetails, `HeadClear`, `HeadGap`, cage and lip sizes).
- **Derived**: computed geometry, do not edit. Includes `Wall`
  (`Nozzle * WallPerims`) and `BrimSegs`, which `tophat_param.py` sets when it
  runs.

`tophat_param.py` rebuilds the document from its own defaults, so put lasting
changes there (or edit the spreadsheet and run only `cage.py`, `export_stl.py`
and `test_kit.py`).

## Design decisions

- **PLA for every part.** Flexures (slider tongues, cage fingers) are sized to
  stay under about 1% bending strain. Exception: the 12 mm slider tongue
  (about 2% by estimate) won the round-3 prints without cracking.
- **Nothing visible outside.** No holes, seams or ports on the crown; all
  cage features are internal. The crown prints top-down so internal features
  on its top never show.
- **Cage loads from below.** The crown flares outward (`Flare`), so its
  bottom opening is its narrowest point. Spring fingers squeeze in 6 mm to
  pass it, then spring out flush to the wall.
- **Rejected cage designs:** straight-sided cage (did not hug the flared wall),
  two-half cage with lap seams (heavier, more parts; dropped for the fingers).
- **Dovetail rails** (45 deg flanks), chosen over a T-rail in the round-2
  prints: slider lips print without overhangs and the slider self-centres.
  Sliders are 12 mm long with a 1.2 mm tongue, chosen from test prints.
- **6 rails**, evenly spaced, starting front and back. Rails stop `HeadGap`
  above a dome model of the head, so they run lower at the sides than the top
  of the head (`HeadClear`).
- **Vertical walls are whole perimeters** (`Wall`, `JointT`, `CollarT`,
  `BandT` = `Nozzle` x a count), so the slicer never leaves gaps or overlaps.
- **Thin crown, thick joint.** The crown wall is `JointT` (7 x 0.4 = 2.8 mm)
  at the brim, where the snap groove and rib slots cut into it, and steps
  to `Wall` (4 x 0.4 = 1.6 mm) in a 45 deg step 1 mm above the rib slots
  (`JointZ`). The step hides under the hatband. Top cap `CapT` 1.2 mm, rib
  slots 0.8 mm deep. Crown is 275 g at the default 7 in (177.8 mm) height.
  The printed hatband is a visual reference; the real hat takes a 38-50 mm
  grosgrain ribbon.
- **Brim segment count is automatic:** the fewest multiple of 4 whose pieces
  fit `Bed`. Multiples of 4 keep seams on both axes (symmetric pieces, and
  the dovetail test coupons stay valid). The crown is never cut (snap
  groove and lip run round it); `tophat_param.py` warns when it does not
  fit, as at XXL on a 220 mm bed.
- **Seams close flush.** On the test prints, bulging printed corners held
  the brim seams open. The dovetail corners are rounded (`KeyR` 1 mm;
  sockets `KeyR` + `Clr`). The seam and key edges on the bed have a 45 deg
  `SeamEF` chamfer for elephant foot. At both seam ends, where the pieces
  end in narrow strips with square corners (collar, edge binding), the seam
  faces stand `SeamGap` back from each other and taper to contact over
  `SeamTaper`. Small notches at the ends were tried first and did not
  help: a notch only makes two new square corners. The keys are built in
  Python when `tophat_param.py` runs, like the binding.
- **Thin brim with a bound edge.** `BrimT` 1.6 mm (8 layers), as in
  cad_tophat, where a brim this thin held its shape. A binding band `RimW`
  6 mm wide stands `RimT` 1.2 mm proud round the whole outer edge, like the
  grosgrain binding on a real top hat. Its profile is swept square to the
  sheet, so the curls end square rather than in the knife edge a vertical
  cut leaves; the top is rounded (`RimR` 1.6 mm), the bottom has a 45 deg
  chamfer (`RimChamfer`) that prints cleanly on the bed. No bulging bead:
  that reads as a bowler or costume hat, not a top hat. Brim is 129 g
  (264 g at 3.8 mm). `CollarH` is a fixed 12 mm so the snap joint does not
  follow `BrimT`.
- **Wedge lip** under the crown top: lead-in taper then a press band that
  squeezes finger tips `LipPress` against the wall (anti-rattle).
