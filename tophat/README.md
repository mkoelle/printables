# 3D-printed top hat with hidden electronics cage

A parametric top hat for a 220 x 220 mm bed, printed in PLA. It hides a mounting
cage for storage and IoT devices above the wearer's head, invisible from
outside. Everything is generated by Python scripts run inside FreeCAD 1.1.

## Parts

| Part | Model | STL | Notes |
|------|-------|-----|-------|
| Crown | `TopHat` | `stl/parts/tophat_crown.stl` | Prints upside down, turned 45 deg. Has snap groove, rib slots and wedge lip inside |
| Brim (`BrimSegs` segments, 4 on a 220 mm bed) | `TopHat` | `stl/parts/tophat_brimq1..N.stl` | Prints on end, standing on its socket seam face: add a slicer plate brim and slicer supports under the curl. Dovetail seams, glued. Inner collar takes the crown. Sides curl up |
| Hatband | none | none | A 38-50 mm grosgrain ribbon; `BandH` and `BandT` keep room for it |
| Cage | `CageFingers` | `stl/parts/tophat_cage_fingers.stl` | Prints upright without support. Bottom ring + 6 spring fingers, each with a toothed dovetail rail that starts in a 45 deg ramp |
| Rail sliders | `lib/rail.py` | `stl/mounts/mount_slider.stl`, `mount_guide.stl` | Click along rails in 2.4 mm steps; the release tab hangs below. Components snap onto one locking slider, plus a tab-less guide slider at the top of tall ones |
| Components | `Mounts` | `stl/mounts/` | Holders that snap onto sliders: breadboard pockets (mini and half size, clipped), rubber-band sleds in three sizes and a bottle holder. See `mounts/README.md` |

Assembly: glue brim segments; fit the hatband ribbon; squeeze the cage finger tips
and push the cage up through the crown's bottom opening; snap the crown onto
the brim collar. The crown's wedge lip then presses the finger tips to the
wall.

## Parameters

The parameters are three lists at the top of `models/hat.py`, the single
source of truth. Each run writes them to the `Params` spreadsheet in
`build/TopHat.FCStd` (generated, not in git), whose formulas compute the advanced and derived values, and
builds every part as a plain shape from those values. Editing the
spreadsheet changes nothing: change the lists and rerun.

- **Main**: `HeadCirc`, `Oval`, `CrownH`, `Flare`, `BrimW`, `Curl`, `Nozzle`,
  `WallPerims`, `Clr`, `Bed`, `BedH` (print height). `HeadCirc` comes from `SIZE` at the top of
  `hat.py`: a preset (XS 530, S 550, M 570, L 590, XL 610, XXL 630 mm)
  or a measured circumference in mm.
- **Advanced**: formulas from Main, overridable (brim, collar, snap, ribs,
  seam key, `HeadClear`, `HeadGap`, cage and lip sizes).
- **Derived**: computed geometry. Includes `Wall` (`Nozzle * WallPerims`),
  the crown flare (`FlareZ`, where the inside starts to flare, and
  `FlareRate`, which `cage.py` also uses) and `BrimSegs`, which
  `hat.py` sets when it runs.

## Design decisions

- **PLA for every part.** Flexures (slider tongues, cage fingers) are sized to
  stay under about 1% bending strain. Exception: the 12 mm slider tongue
  (about 2.3% by estimate) won the round-3 prints without cracking.
- **Nothing visible outside.** No holes, seams or ports on the crown; all
  cage features are internal. The crown prints top-down so internal features
  on its top never show.
- **Cage loads from below.** The crown flares outward (`Flare`), so its
  bottom opening is its narrowest point. Spring fingers squeeze in 6 mm to
  pass it, then spring out flush to the wall.
- **Rejected cage designs:** straight-sided cage (did not hug the flared wall),
  two-half cage with lap seams (heavier, more parts; dropped for the fingers).
- **Dovetail rails** (45 deg flanks), chosen over a T-rail in the round-2
  prints: slider lips print without overhangs and the slider self-centres.
  Sliders are 12 mm long with a 1.4 mm tongue, chosen from test prints.
- **6 rails**, evenly spaced, starting front and back. Rails stop `HeadGap`
  above a dome model of the head, so they run lower at the sides than the top
  of the head (`HeadClear`).
- **Vertical walls are whole perimeters** (`Wall`, `JointT`, `CollarT` =
  `Nozzle` x a count, and `BrimT` = 4 x 0.4 mm, vertical when the pieces
  print on end), so the slicer never leaves gaps or overlaps.
- **Thin crown, thick joint.** The crown wall is `JointT` (7 x 0.4 = 2.8 mm)
  at the brim, where the snap groove and rib slots cut into it, and steps
  to `Wall` (4 x 0.4 = 1.6 mm) in a 45 deg step 1 mm above the rib slots
  (`JointZ`). The step hides under the hatband. Top cap `CapT` 1.2 mm, rib
  slots 0.8 mm deep. Crown is 275 g at the default 7 in (177.8 mm) height.
  The hatband is a 38-50 mm grosgrain ribbon, not printed (a printed
  reference band was dropped).
- **Brim segment count is automatic:** the fewest multiple of 4 whose pieces
  fit the printer standing on end (`Bed` long, `BedH` tall; at M a piece is
  174 mm long and 166 mm tall). Multiples of 4 keep seams on both axes
  (symmetric pieces, and the dovetail test coupon stays valid). The crown
  is never cut (snap groove and lip run round it); `hat.py` fails
  its crown fit check when it does not fit, as at XXL on a 220 mm bed.
- **Seams close flush.** Bulging printed corners held the first test seams
  open, so the brim key and seam were reworked over several coupon prints:
  - **Key:** a dovetail whose root and head roundings (`KeyRootR` 2 mm,
    `KeyHeadR` 3.4 mm) nearly meet, so each flank is an S of two exact arcs
    and no corner is sharp. Clearance `KeyClr` 0.1 mm, tighter than `Clr`:
    the curved flanks give about 2.6 x the clearance as pull-apart play, and
    at 0.2 mm the key printed loose. At 0.1 mm (about 0.25 mm play) it held.
  - **Tried and dropped:** small end notches (they only make new corners),
    bed-edge chamfers (`SeamEF`, for pieces printed flat),
    set-backs at the collar end and at the edge binding, extra room past
    the key head, a plain rounded dovetail (looser), a spline S-curve key
    (held, but fragile code) and an S-curve with relief everywhere but
    under the head.
  - The key is built in Python by `seam.py` when `hat.py` runs.
- **Brim pieces print on end.** A piece stood on a seam face printed
  cleanly with a slicer plate brim and slicer supports under the curl.
  `export.py` writes each brim STL standing on its socket seam face.
  Printed flat, the curl underside starts as a ceiling just above the bed
  and strings or welds to supports; built-in breakaway fins and a foot for
  that were tried and removed once the on-end print worked. If the tongue
  binds at the bed end of the socket, turn on the slicer's elephant-foot
  compensation.
- **Thin brim with a bound edge.** `BrimT` 1.6 mm (4 perimeters), as in
  cad_tophat, where a brim this thin held its shape. A binding band `RimW`
  6 mm wide stands `RimT` 1.2 mm proud round the whole outer edge, like the
  grosgrain binding on a real top hat. Its profile is swept square to the
  sheet, so the curls end square rather than in the knife edge a vertical
  cut leaves; the top is rounded (`RimR` 1.6 mm), the bottom outer edge has
  a 45 deg chamfer (`RimChamfer`) that softens it. No bulging bead:
  that reads as a bowler or costume hat, not a top hat. Brim is 129 g
  (264 g at 3.8 mm). `CollarH` is a fixed 12 mm so the snap joint does not
  follow `BrimT`.
- **Wedge lip** under the crown top: lead-in taper then a press band that
  squeezes finger tips `LipPress` against the wall (anti-rattle).

## Status (2026-11-06)

Size M, 7 in tall: crown 275 g, brim 4 x 32 g, cage 117 g.
All parts model as valid, single solids and fit a 220 mm bed and height.

Test prints so far:

- **Round 1**: head fit, dovetail seam, T-rail and sliders.
  The sliders were too tight and the spring tongue snapped at its root.
- **Round 2**: reworked sliders on a T-rail and a dovetail rail. The
  dovetail rail was chosen.
- **Round 3**: 12 mm sliders won (1.4 mm tongue, sample 3). The brim seam coupon with
  rounded keys still stood open: corners bulged at both seam ends. Later
  rounds settled the key (see Design decisions).
- **Since**: the joint test pieces, a full crown and the collar ring fit
  well; seam coupon 5 held; a brim piece printed well on end.

Next:

- Print the lip ring with a finger tip. See `TESTING.md`.
- Then print the brim and the cage.

Components: first pass designed (breadboard pockets, sleds, bottle holder); the slider has
new side grooves for them. Next, print the shoe coupon. See `mounts/README.md`.

Open options: mid-height cage ring if the fingers flex under load.
